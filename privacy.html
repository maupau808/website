Privacy Policy

Updated October 27, 2026

Maui Power Equipment, Inc. uses the contact details, equipment information, and messages you provide to respond to inquiries and handle parts requests, orders, and repairs. Our website also uses basic visit analytics to understand how it is used.

Information sharing

We use service providers to operate our website, deliver form submissions, and handle communications. We do not sell your personal information.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. Text-message opt-in records and consent are not shared with third parties for their own use.

Text messages

If you opt in, we may text you about your inquiry, parts, orders, or repairs. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. You can buy from us without signing up for texts.
